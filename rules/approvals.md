# Approvals and outward writes

- **Draft first.** Linear tickets, comments, messages and anything else visible outside the team are shown to the human as an editable draft. The human can edit before anything is filed.
- **One approval, one write.** Approval of a draft covers that write only, not the session.
- **Priorities.** Suggest a priority only with a stated reason, and do not inflate it. The human can edit it.
- **No uploads.** In the app, steps that need document uploads are done by the human. Skip them and move on.
- **Shared drives are read-only.** Never modify, move or download whole files from shared project folders. Look through them and link the closest document.
- **Data.** No writes to production or customer data. Data fixes go through audited application endpoints, never direct writes.
- **Test data only** in the app: the dev environment and its test organization.
