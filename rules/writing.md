# Writing rules (all human-readable output)

- Concise. No filler. Every claim anchored to a line, a record, a ticket or a document.
- When citing a document, say what it is and where; include a link when it is a shared document.
- State gaps plainly. Say "not found" rather than guessing.
- Separate what was observed from what is inferred.
- Plain words. No hype.
